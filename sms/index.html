SMS Terms

What this program is

Jeremy Schwach's assistant, Grokkie, sends and receives low-volume, one-to-one text messages from +1 (608) 299-9876. Messages are about scheduling and appointment confirmations, logistics and reminders, replies to questions you asked, and one-time verification codes for people who phoned this number and asked for one. There are no promotional or bulk messages.

Who receives messages and how you opt in

Recipients are Jeremy's family members, his executive assistant, and a small number of vendors, schools and service providers who asked to be reached by text. There is no web sign-up form. You opt in in one of these ways:

  • You text +1 (608) 299-9876 first, or reply to a text from it; or
  • You tell Jeremy Schwach in person, by phone, or in writing (email or text) that you agree to text with this number, and give or confirm your own mobile number.

After you agree, the first message you receive is a confirmation: “You're now set up to text with Jeremy Schwach's assistant, Grokkie. Msg frequency varies, usually a few per month. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.” No one is texted before consent is given. Consent is not a condition of buying anything.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per month per person.

Costs

Msg & data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge for messages according to your plan.

How to opt out (STOP)

Reply STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT) at any time. You will get one confirmation and no further messages. To resume, reply START. You can also tell Jeremy directly.

How to get help (HELP)

Reply HELP or INFO to the text line, email jeremy@schwachendeavors.com, or call +1 (608) 299-9876.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is not guaranteed.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your mobile phone number, your name (if you give it), the content of messages you exchange with this line, the date and manner of your opt-in or opt-out, and delivery records kept by our messaging provider.

How we use it

Only to send and receive the messages described above, to keep a record of your consent and opt-out choices, and to maintain Jeremy's personal contact records.

No sale or sharing for marketing

Mobile phone numbers and opt-in data are not shared with or sold to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Information is shared only with service providers who help deliver messages and operate the assistant (for example, our telecommunications provider, Telnyx, and the AI and software tools that process messages on Jeremy's behalf). They may use it only to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Retention and security

Message records are kept only as long as needed to run the line and keep consent records, and are protected with access controls. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop messages. To ask what we hold about you or have it deleted, email jeremy@schwachendeavors.com.
